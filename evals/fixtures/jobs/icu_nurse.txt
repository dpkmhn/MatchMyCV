Registered Nurse — Intensive Care Unit (Band 6)
St Aldric's University Hospital, Leeds — Full time, rotating shifts

The 24-bed adult ICU cares for medical, surgical and trauma patients. We are looking for an experienced
nurse to lead shifts, support junior staff and deliver high-quality critical care.

Responsibilities
- Provide one-to-one and one-to-two care for critically ill ventilated patients
- Titrate vasoactive infusions and respond to deteriorating patients
- Act as nurse in charge on rotation and mentor newly qualified nurses
- Document care accurately in the electronic patient record

Essential
- Registered Nurse with active NMC registration
- Minimum 2 years of ICU or critical care experience
- Current Basic Life Support (BLS) certification
- Advanced Life Support (ALS) certification
- Experience with Epic electronic health records

Desirable
- Post-registration critical care course or CCRN certification
- Experience managing mechanically ventilated patients
- Mentorship or practice supervisor qualification
