Senior Data Analyst — Cartwheel Retail, Manchester (Hybrid)

About us
Cartwheel runs 140 grocery and homeware stores across the North of England and a growing online shop.
The analytics team turns sales, loyalty and supply data into decisions for merchandising and marketing.

What you'll do
- Own weekly trading reports for category managers and the leadership team
- Design and analyse A/B tests for the website and loyalty offers
- Build and maintain dashboards that replace manual spreadsheet reporting
- Present findings to non-technical stakeholders and recommend actions

Requirements
- 5+ years of experience in a data analyst or similar analytics role
- Advanced SQL (window functions, CTEs, query performance)
- Experience with Tableau or Power BI
- Python or R for analysis
- Experience designing and analysing A/B tests
- Strong communication skills and experience presenting to senior stakeholders

Nice to have
- dbt
- Snowflake
- Experience in retail or e-commerce
