Associate Product Manager — Lumio Learning, London

Lumio builds a reading app used by 400 primary schools. Teachers set reading, pupils read and answer
questions, and the app adapts to each child.

What you'll do
- Talk to teachers and pupils every week and turn what you learn into clear problem statements
- Write product requirements and work with designers and engineers to ship features
- Prioritise the roadmap for the teacher dashboard
- Define success metrics and track them after each release

Requirements
- 1+ years of product management experience
- Experience running user research interviews
- Ability to write clear product requirement documents
- Comfortable with data: SQL or a product analytics tool such as Amplitude or Mixpanel
- Experience working with agile software teams

Nice to have
- Background in education or edtech
- Experience with Figma
