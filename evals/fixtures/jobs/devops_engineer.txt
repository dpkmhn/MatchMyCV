DevOps Engineer — Halberd Payments, Remote (UK)

Halberd processes card payments for 3,000 small businesses. The platform team keeps 60 services running
around the clock and makes it easy for product teams to ship safely.

You will
- Run and improve our Kubernetes clusters on AWS (EKS)
- Manage infrastructure as code with Terraform
- Own CI/CD pipelines and help teams deploy many times a day
- Build monitoring and alerting with Prometheus and Grafana
- Join the on-call rotation and lead incident reviews

Must have
- 3+ years in a DevOps, SRE or platform engineering role
- Production experience with Kubernetes
- Terraform
- AWS
- CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions, GitLab CI or Jenkins)
- Monitoring with Prometheus and Grafana
- Strong Linux administration skills
- On-call experience

Nice to have
- Go
- Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA)
- PCI DSS experience
